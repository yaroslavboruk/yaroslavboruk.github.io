About

I work at the intersection of music production, audio engineering, and live event management — disciplines developed and applied in parallel across international markets since 2023. Every system built from scratch, every process owned end-to-end.

On the audio side: full pipeline from composition and sound design through mixing, mastering, and distribution. On the events side: 10+ productions across three countries — venue acquisition, artist contracting, technical coordination, digital and offline marketing, and financial oversight. Both disciplines inform each other.

I value precision, long-term thinking, and conceptual integrity. Autonomous by default, ownership-oriented, and drawn to work with genuine artistic or cultural stakes.
